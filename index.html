Metric definitions and reporting coverage

The Overall tab adds up every court with civil and criminal cases combined and charts each court against the per-court average. The Civil and Criminal tabs compare a primary court with a comparison court on monthly workload, case mix and outcomes within one division.

Assumptions: each court has reported either all or none of its civil and criminal values for a given reporting period. Totals assume all courts have reported; the status line flags any month where a court’s data is missing.

Active and inactive pending are month-end snapshots. Clearance rate is total dispositions divided by new filings and can exceed 100%. It is undefined when filings are zero. With All courts selected, counts are summed and clearance rate uses total dispositions divided by total filings. Appeals combine appeals after trial and without trial. Disposition categories and totals are displayed as reported.

The combined extract covers September 2011 through September 2026. Division-wide metrics use the reported All cases rows. Civil case-type labels differ across reporting periods. A case type absent from a month is shown as unavailable.

Source: Texas OCA combined civil and criminal extract · September 2011–September 2026 · Harris County only
