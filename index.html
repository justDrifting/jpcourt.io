Metric definitions and reporting coverage

Active and inactive pending are month-end snapshots. Clearance rate is total dispositions divided by new filings and can exceed 100%. It is undefined when filings are zero. With All courts selected, counts are summed and clearance rate uses total dispositions divided by total filings. The countywide average is the simple average of court values for the selected case type and month, including the courts selected for comparison. A court counts if it reported any positive case count or activity for the selected case type that month. This includes cases already pending. Missing case-type records are excluded. For clearance rate, each court with at least one new filing counts equally. Appeals combine appeals after trial and without trial. Disposition categories and totals are displayed as reported.

The combined extract covers September 2011 through September 2026. Division-wide metrics use the reported All cases rows. Civil case-type labels differ across reporting periods. A case type absent from a month is shown as unavailable.
